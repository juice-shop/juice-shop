Why AI challenges?

LLMs are everywhere and bring a brand-new attack surface

Juice Shop now lets you hands-on explore and exploit the kind of flaws described in the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications. The legacy NLP-based chatbot and its challenges were retired to make room for these.

Presenter: Jannik

Bring your own LLM

The AI challenges need a configured LLM/AI endpoint

🖥️ Local Offline models via Ollama Completely free of charge but performance limited depending on your local machine
☁️ Remote OpenAI-compatible servers (e.g. OpenRouter) for hosted models You can choose between pay-as-you-go models or rate-limited free models

Juice Shop will check the configured LLM/AI endpoint during server startup and show whether it is reachable in the respective challenge cards on the Score Board.

Presenter: Jannik

Spam PR Statistics

Juice Shop enacts a strict no-spam-policy to keep the project clean

Until 2024, most spam PRs came from user mistakes when "playing around" with tools & scanners. With AI tools gaining popularity, the project has been receiving an increasing number of spam PRs with auto-generated content.
